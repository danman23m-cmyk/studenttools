Final Grade Calculator

Find the score you need on a final exam, assignment, or other remaining coursework to reach your target class grade.

How to use this final grade calculator

  1. Enter your current class grade.
  2. Enter how much the remaining exam, assignment, or coursework is worth.
  3. Enter the final grade you want to achieve.
  4. Select Calculate Needed Score.

What grade do I need on my final?

This calculator determines the score you need on your remaining coursework to reach a desired overall class grade. It works by weighting your current grade by the portion of the course already completed and then determining what score is required on the remaining work.

Final grade formula

Needed Score = (Desired Grade − Current Grade × Completed Weight) ÷ Remaining Weight
Completed Weight = 1 − Remaining Weight

Final grade calculation example

Suppose your current grade is 82%, your final exam is worth 25% of your course grade, and you want to finish with a 90%.

(90 − 82 × 0.75) ÷ 0.25 = 114%

In this example, you would need a 114% on the final, which means a 90% overall course grade is not mathematically reachable from the values entered.

What if I need more than 100%?

If the calculator says you need more than 100%, your target final grade cannot be reached with the remaining coursework under the numbers you entered. You can still use the calculator to determine the highest possible final course grade.

What if I need less than 0%?

If the required score is below 0%, you have already earned enough points to reach your target based on the values entered. You should still complete any required coursework according to your course policies.

Final exam vs. remaining coursework

Despite the name, this calculator does not only work for final exams. You can use it for a final project, assignment, presentation, test, or any other remaining coursework when you know its percentage of the total course grade.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate the grade I need on my final?

Multiply your current grade by the percentage of the course already completed, subtract that result from your desired final grade, and divide by the percentage remaining.

What if the calculator says I need more than 100%?

If the required score is above 100%, the target final grade is not mathematically reachable with the remaining coursework under the values entered.

Can I use this calculator for a final exam?

Yes. Enter your current grade, the percentage of your course grade represented by the final exam, and your desired final course grade.

Can I use this for an assignment instead of a final exam?

Yes. The calculator can be used for any remaining coursework as long as you know its percentage of the total course grade.

What happens if I enter 100% as the remaining work?

If 100% of the course remains, the required score is the same as the desired final grade.
