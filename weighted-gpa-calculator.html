How to Calculate a Weighted GPA

A credit-weighted GPA takes both your course GPA and the number of credit hours for each course into account.

Weighted GPA Formula

Weighted GPA = Σ (Course GPA × Course Credits) ÷ Total Credits

Example

Suppose you have three courses:

Course 1: 4.0 GPA × 3 credits = 12.0 quality points

Course 2: 3.0 GPA × 3 credits = 9.0 quality points

Course 3: 3.7 GPA × 4 credits = 14.8 quality points

Total quality points = 35.8

Total credits = 10

35.8 ÷ 10 = 3.58 weighted GPA

How to Use This Calculator

Enter the GPA value and credit hours for each course. Then select Calculate Weighted GPA to calculate your credit-weighted GPA.

Courses with more credit hours have a larger effect on the final GPA than courses with fewer credit hours.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a weighted GPA?

A weighted GPA accounts for the number of credit hours associated with each course. A higher-credit course has a larger effect on the overall GPA.

How do you calculate a weighted GPA?

Multiply each course GPA by its credit hours, add those values together, and divide by the total number of credit hours.

Why do credit hours affect GPA?

Credit hours indicate how much coursework a class represents. Because of this, a course with more credits contributes more to a credit-weighted GPA.

Can I use this for college courses?

Yes. Enter the GPA value and credit hours for each college course to estimate your credit-weighted GPA.

What if my school's GPA scale is different?

This calculator uses the GPA values you enter. Schools may use different grading scales or policies, so check your school's official GPA rules if you need an exact institutional calculation.
