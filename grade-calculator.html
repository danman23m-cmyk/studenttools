Calculate your grade percentage from points earned and total points.

How to Calculate Your Grade

To calculate your grade percentage, divide the points you earned by the total possible points and multiply by 100.

Grade Percentage Formula

Grade Percentage = (Points Earned ÷ Total Points) × 100

Example

If you earned 85 points out of 100 possible points:

(85 ÷ 100) × 100 = 85%

Your grade would be 85%.

How to Use This Calculator

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate my grade percentage?

Divide your points earned by the total possible points and multiply by 100. For example, 85 out of 100 points equals 85%.

What is the formula for calculating a grade?

The formula is: (points earned ÷ total points) × 100.

Can I use different point totals?

Yes. You can enter any earned-point and total-point values, such as 42 out of 50 or 178 out of 200.

What if I earned more than the total points?

A percentage above 100% can occur when extra credit is included.
